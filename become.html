Become a Tutor

Share your knowledge and help students build confidence, practical skills, and new opportunities. Whether you have a set currciulum in mind, or would need some more support, Path to Parallel will provide an in-depth resource and lessons packet to you. To become a tutor, we will have a team member conduct an interview with you to get started and get to know you! In this meeting, we will also go over future initiatives and goals, including the possibility of future tutoring packets of resources to use during lessons. This is not currently a paid position, but we can provide volunteer hours. Once involved, we also offer opportunities for professional development and networking and opportunity for more roles within the foundation.

Tell us a little about yourself and the subjects you would like to support.
